Graceful Degradation and service workers are related because they both are utilized to maintain usuability when ideal conditions fail, but they cover different areas. Graceful degradation is a more general term that allows a system to still be functional but in a limited way even is something major goes wrong, such as a feature failing or being unavailable. Service workers allows a webpage/app too still function even if there is no or slow internet connection or if the server is unavailable, this being possible through local caches and altering requests. Service workers fall under the term general degradation.
